# Targeted claim verification

Identify the exact manuscript claim and cited paper. Find the supporting or contradictory source passage, figure/table, and relevant methods, controls, or supplementary evidence. Read beyond the cited section when needed to resolve ambiguity; do not require an unrelated full-paper artifact package.

For each claim record: source identity and version; exact section/page/figure/table anchor; original analytical explanation or a short permitted excerpt; matched species, genotype, tissue, stage, treatment, and environment; evidence type; support grade; limitations and a justified correction when needed.

Support grades are `strong`, `partial`, `background`, `contradictory`, `metadata-only`, and `not-supported`. Distinguish direct evidence from the authors' interpretation and your inference. Bibliographic existence alone does not validate a biological or causal claim. If the source is incomplete, specify what was available and which conclusions remain unresolved.

Return a concise evidence-backed verdict, or a table for a batch. Do not create a mind map, complete bilingual note, JIF lookup, domain update, or asset-library entry unless requested. Reuse an existing verified record when its source version and intended claim still match. If invoked during writing, store useful records with that project's intermediate citation audit.
