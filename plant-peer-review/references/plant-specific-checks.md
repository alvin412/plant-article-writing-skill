# Plant-science review checks / 植物科学专业审稿

## Research manuscript
- Botanical identity, species/cultivar/accession/genetic background, tissue, developmental stage, environment, stress dose and sampling time.
- Independent transgenic or edited lines; wild type, empty-vector, segregation and complementation controls where relevant.
- Biological vs technical replicates; plant/pot/plot/chamber/field randomization level; nested observations, blocks and batch effects.
- Expression association vs genetic necessity/sufficiency vs direct molecular binding/regulation vs physiological mechanism vs field agronomic effect. Demand evidence matched to each causal word.
- qRT-PCR reference genes, RNA-seq batch processing and multiple tests, protein interactions with appropriate controls, microscopy calibration/scales and image provenance, field G×E and yield tradeoffs when relevant.
- Gene/locus, transcript, protein, mutant, allele and species nomenclature are context-specific, not interchangeable.
- Figures: raw observations, individual data when feasible, units, error-bar definition, n, multiple comparisons, panel letters and source provenance.

## Review manuscript
- Search boundaries and recency; compare primary studies not just prior reviews; inclusion of negative and conflicting evidence.
- Cross-species extrapolation, abiotic and biotic stress differences, controlled vs field conditions, developmental and tissue specificity.
- Strength of mechanistic vs associational evidence; literature bias; synthesis originality; systematic/scoping reporting standards only when relevant.
- Do not transfer findings from a model plant directly to a crop without marking uncertainty.

## Reviewer-style questions
1. What is the strongest conclusion justified by this exact experiment or cited paper?
2. What rival explanation survives every currently reported control?
3. Which evidence is essential, which is supplementary, and which repeats earlier figures?
4. Which result would overturn the central claim?
5. Would a reanalysis or honest claim restriction address the issue without unnecessary new experiments?
