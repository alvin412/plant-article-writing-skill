# Review protocol / 审稿流程

## 1. Intake and freeze
Record manuscript ID/version, original-research or review type, requested scope, language, target journal if known, bibliography, figures, source data, supplement, and accessible evidence. Re-review must preserve the prior issue IDs and compare immutable versions.

## 2. Review dimensions
- Significance, conceptual novelty **relative to checked literature**, meaningful contribution, scope and target readership.
- Scientific logic: research question, claims, alternative interpretations, contradictions, and whether the take-home message follows.
- Original research: study design, experimental unit, treatments, controls, randomization, blocking, data quality, reproducibility, statistical analysis, figures, Methods/Results agreement.
- Review articles: search coverage and reported limits, representative evidence, treatment of contradictory work, cross-study synthesis, review-type reporting, contribution beyond recent reviews.
- Writing: logical paragraph roles, terminology, taxonomic and gene/protein nomenclature, English and academic-Chinese scientific equivalence.

## 3. Parallel perspectives when *actually possible*
Provide an identical frozen source packet and specific non-overlapping review emphasis to three isolated review invocations. Do not transmit their preliminary findings to the other invocations. Freeze reports, then do a separate cross-review aggregation. If isolation cannot be implemented, label output "multi-perspective AI self-review" and never "blind independent reviewers". Do not claim an external editorial decision.

## 4. Forensic pass
Outside reviewer perspectives, check explicit numeric values, directions and units; Results vs Abstract/Discussion/figure captions/supplements; n vs number of measurements; statistics labels and adjusted tests; citation/source and plant-material conditions. If source files are missing, list needed inputs instead of claiming checks passed.

## 5. Synthesis and revision
Prioritize critical blockers by impact on central claim. Do not invent minor issues to balance a report. For each issue propose: (a) re-analysis/reporting correction, (b) bounded claim reduction, (c) essential experiment, or (d) justified disagreement. Produce an issue ledger and revision sequence. Let the author decide whether to run new experiments.

## 6. Re-review and stop
For each original issue, inspect exact revised location and evidence; mark RESOLVED, PARTIAL, OPEN, or NOT_ASSESSABLE. Stop review iterations when no material issues remain under the accessible evidence or author opts to stop; never certify correctness beyond evaluated materials.
