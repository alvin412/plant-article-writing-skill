# Integrated Target-Journal Finalization Checklist

## Delivery scope

For journal-finalization-only work, apply requirements to the supplied manuscript in its requested language and file format. Deliver the revised manuscript and requested compliance findings; do not create a Chinese translation or four-file package unless requested. The bilingual filenames and complete package below apply only to full-manuscript/full-package mode. Missing journal requirements prevent a submission-ready verdict but do not prevent useful corrections whose basis is already known.

## 1. Required Inputs and Version Freeze

Before finalization, identify the exact revised manuscript version, target journal, article type, official author instructions, template, reference style, figures, tables, supplements, declarations, and reference data. The journal may remain undecided during drafting. Missing authoritative requirements prevent a submission-ready verdict; deliver verified corrections and clearly identify remaining requirements. Never guess a journal rule.

## 2. Requirement Sources

Prefer the journal's official author instructions, article-type page, reference guide, manuscript template, reporting checklist, and submission-system guidance. Record the source URL or supplied file, access date, applicable article type, and conflicts between sources. Use the newest authoritative requirement and disclose unresolved conflicts.

## 3. Integrated Manuscript Contract

For the complete bilingual package, finalize the English portion of `01_plant_article_bilingual.docx` and retain its Chinese portion for the author. For journal-only work, finalize the supplied manuscript in the requested language and file; create no additional translation or review package.

Treat the English portion as the frozen submission manuscript for compliance checks. Keep the English and Chinese scientific meaning aligned, but do not force journal-specific English typography or layout onto the Chinese portion unless needed for consistency or readability.

## 4. Structure and References

Check title page, author and affiliation format, corresponding author, abstract type and length, keywords, heading hierarchy, section order, word limits, abbreviations, acknowledgements, references, figures, tables, supplements, required declarations, anonymization, line numbering, and file conventions.

Verify bibliography metadata before formatting. Check in-text and bibliography correspondence, ordering, punctuation, author truncation, journal abbreviations, DOI presentation, and treatment of preprints, datasets, software, and websites. Formatting cannot rescue an unsupported citation; record substantive support problems in the claim-citation audit and block submission when material.

## 5. Scientific Nomenclature and English

Create an authoritative terminology ledger. Apply journal and organism-specific rules for species names, gene and allele symbols, proteins, mutants, genotypes, transgenes, constructs, transcripts, loci, haplotypes, Greek letters, superscripts, subscripts, units, statistical symbols, and chemical notation across text, tables, figures, legends, and supplements. Apply italics as Word character formatting rather than markdown markers.

Apply the journal's spelling, punctuation, capitalization, abbreviations, number style, tense, voice, and terminology. Remove ambiguity, repetition, unsupported promotional language, and claim inflation without changing verified scientific meaning.

## 6. Figures, Tables, and Declarations

Check format, resolution, dimensions, colour mode, panel labels, fonts, legends, callouts, numbering, units, sample sizes, error bars, tests, significance notation, image integrity, and source data. Verify Data Availability, Code Availability, CRediT contributions, funding, conflicts, ethics, acknowledgements, AI disclosure, and supplementary descriptions when applicable.

## 7. Bilingual Compliance Report

For the full bilingual package, create `04_journal_compliance_audit_bilingual.docx`. For journal-only work, provide the requested compliance findings in the requested format and language. Include manuscript identity and frozen version, official sources, a requirement-by-requirement matrix, reference and nomenclature audit, gene/protein typography changes, language and structural changes, figure/table/declaration checks, material change ledger, unresolved `WARN` and `BLOCK` items, rendering status, and the final submission-readiness decision.

Never claim full compliance without current authoritative instructions and completed visual QA. If a blocking requirement remains, mark the English manuscript portion and compliance report as not submission-ready and identify the exact blocker.
