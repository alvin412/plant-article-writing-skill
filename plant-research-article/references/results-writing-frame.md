## User-Protected Results-Writing Rhetorical Frame

This is a permanent user-defined invariant. Every future edit, refactor, consolidation, or version update of this skill must preserve the following rhetorical frame and its meaning. Do not delete, weaken, replace, or supersede it unless the user explicitly requests its removal or revision.

When drafting a logically independent result unit, adapt the following purpose-driven frame to the actual scientific question, experiment, evidence strength, and paragraph context:

- Chinese: `为了了解/明确/解析/阐明……，我们进行了……实验。结果显示/表明/揭示……。`
- English: `To understand/determine/resolve/elucidate ..., we performed/conducted ... . The results showed/indicated/revealed/demonstrated that ... .`

Preserve the underlying logic of `research purpose -> experiment or analysis -> principal observation -> quantitative and statistical evidence -> figure or table anchor -> bounded conclusion`, but do not mechanically repeat the same opening or sentence pattern across paragraphs. Reorder, merge, or expand the elements when the article's evidence chain and narrative flow require it. Select purpose and result verbs according to the study content and strength of evidence; reserve mechanistic or causal verbs such as `解析/阐明`, `elucidate`, and `demonstrated` for evidence that genuinely resolves the mechanism or supports the causal claim. This protected frame supplements, and never overrides, the prohibition on invented data or interpretation beyond the evidence.
